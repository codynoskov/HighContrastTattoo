Hey, I’m Chris. I started my tattoo journey at High Contrast Tattoo and have now been tattooing for over a year. For me, tattooing is about more than creating an image on skin. I want people to feel comfortable in their bodies and to use tattoos as a way to express something personal, without feeling limited by gender, beauty standards or expectations from other people.

My style is mainly illustrative blackwork, using clean linework, strong black areas and sometimes small colour accents, often pink. I started drawing through manga and anime, and those influences are still visible in the way I build characters, movement and expression. I especially enjoy tattooing birds, animals, plants and other lifeforms, but I also like creating more abstract botanical tattoos and unusual custom designs with a slightly surreal or experimental feeling.

Most important to me is finding a design and placement that works naturally with your body. Whether the tattoo is small and subtle or larger and more expressive, I want it to feel like it belongs there. During sessions, I want people of all bodies and genders to feel welcome and comfortable. If you need a more private setup, specific accommodations or would like me to use certain pronouns, just let me know and I’ll be happy to make the session work for you.
