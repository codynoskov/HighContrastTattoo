Color tattoos can take many different forms, from simple graphic designs and stylized illustrations to animals, characters, plants, objects and abstract compositions. This category brings together colorful tattoo work that does not clearly belong to one of the more specific tattoo styles on our website.

Colour can completely change the feeling of a tattoo. It can make a design playful, soft, bold, unusual or highly graphic, depending on the palette and composition. Different colours can separate shapes, create contrast, add depth and highlight important details. A color tattoo can be minimal and simple or become a larger, more detailed custom composition.

If your tattoo idea does not fit clearly into styles such as Traditional, Neo Traditional, Anime, Floral or another specific category, but colour is an important part of the design, it will usually fit here. Our artists create custom color tattoos based on your ideas, references and preferred colour palettes.
